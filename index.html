HOW TO WIN

  • Pick 3 houses with your 999G budget.
  • Strength = total power (Tier 1 = 3, Tier 2 = 2, Tier 3 = 1).
  • Attack beats Mix, Mix beats Defense, Defense beats Attack: every winning matchup between two houses gives +5% strength, every losing one -5%.
  • Each side then rolls luck between 0.85x and 1.15x.
  • On Hard the enemy picks the alliance that counters yours.
